# Book Reviews

## Book Review September 14, 2026

### Brain Maker: The Power of Gut Microbes to Heal and Protect Your Brain for Life by David Perlmutter and Kristin Loberg

Genre: Non-fiction, Health
Publication Year:  2015 (US)
Publisher: Little, Brown and Company 

Brain Maker draws on scientific evidence both from experimental research and popular scientific literature. Since the book’s publication in 2015, numerous population-based and experimental studies have further highlighted correlations between the gut microbiome and brain, gastrointestinal, and overall health. However, it is important to distinguish between correlation and causation, especially in microbiome research. Also, his enthusiasm and predictions about fecal microbiota transplantation as a potential treatment for a range of disorders, have been more compelling than the subsequent evidence has demonstrated. Despite these limitations, Brain Maker remains a useful introduction to the rapidly evolving field of the gut microbiome.
